Frequently Asked Questions

How long does refund processing take? ▼

Refunds typically take 3-5 business days to appear in your original payment method. This depends on your bank or card issuer's processing time. We initiate the refund immediately upon cancellation.

What if my refund fails? ▼

If your refund fails, our support team will be notified automatically. We'll contact you within 24 hours to resolve the issue. You can also contact us directly through the support section in the refund status card.

Can I check my refund status in multiple ways? ▼

Yes! You can track your refund status on this page, and we'll also send you notifications via email and push notifications. You'll receive an alert as soon as your refund is credited.

What is the cancellation refund policy? ▼

We offer full refunds for cancellations made up to 24 hours before your pickup date. Cancellations within 24 hours may have a deduction for processing fees. Check your refund details for the exact amount.
